Frequently Asked Questions

What are the prerequisites for CUA?

There are no strict prerequisites. CUA is designed for working professionals with some exposure to UX, product design, or digital strategy. A background in design, technology, or business is helpful but not required.

Is CUA fully online?

Yes. CUA is delivered fully online, with live sessions and self-paced components you can complete from anywhere.

How is CUA different from other UX certifications?

CUA is grounded in the science of usability and human factors, with a rigorous curriculum and global recognition. It emphasizes practical application and is backed by HFI's legacy in the field.

How long is certification valid?

Your CUA certification does not expire. We recommend staying current with practice and optional continuing education.

What is the refund policy?

Refund terms depend on when you withdraw. Contact us or refer to your enrollment agreement for the current policy.

Do you offer corporate discounts?

Yes. We offer volume and corporate pricing for teams. Get in touch to discuss your needs.
