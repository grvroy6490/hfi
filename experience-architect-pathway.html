A Three-Stage Capability Model

Progress from foundational practice to organizational leadership in experience.

  1. Stage 1 - Foundation

    CUA

    Certified Usability Architect

    Establish usability as a disciplined, evidence-led foundation for experience quality.

    You Learn To

    • Ground decisions in human cognition and behavior
    • Evaluate and improve usability as a system-level responsibility
    • Build trust, accessibility, and adoption through evidence-based design

    Outcome

    Usability Architect

    Start with CUA

  2. Stage 2 - Advancement

    CDPA

    Certified Digital Persuasion Architect

    Design and govern ethical influence across journeys and ecosystems.

    You Learn To

    • Architect influence across end-to-end experiences
    • Apply PET-based models to trust and decision-making
    • Balance growth, engagement, and ethics in AI-driven environments

    Outcome

    Digital Persuasion Architect

    Continue to CDPA

  3. Stage 3 - Leadership

    CXA

    Certified Experience Architect

    Shape experience as a strategic, organization-wide capability.

    You Learn To

    • Define experience vision and maturity roadmaps
    • Establish governance and operating models
    • Lead experience strategy with business and technology leadership

    Outcome

    Experience Architect

    Become CXA
